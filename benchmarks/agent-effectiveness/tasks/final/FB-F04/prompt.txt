Sanitized rich text may keep links that open in a new tab, but those links must not retain access to the opener. Ensure every sanitized anchor with `target="_blank"` has both `noopener` and `noreferrer` in its `rel` value, while preserving any existing safe relation tokens. Anchors with other targets must not be changed just because they have a target. Existing script, event-handler, and inline-style sanitization must continue to work.
